FIELD MANUAL

Stay alive. Get paid.

Movement

WASD to move, SHIFT to sprint, SPACE to jump. Sprinting drains stamina; it regenerates when you slow down.

Gunplay

Hold LMB for full-auto. Tap RMB to aim and tighten spread. Recoil climbs — pull down and pace your bursts. R reloads.

Loot & cargo

Walk over scrap and stims to collect them. Mine cyan Ether-Core nodes and crack crates with E. You can carry 24 kg; cores weigh 3 kg each.

Extraction

Lift 04 sits northeast of the refinery. Hold E on the pad to call it, then stay close for twelve seconds. Any hit taken during ascent does not stop the lift.

Death

If integrity hits zero, your cargo is gone and the run ends. You respawn at Sanctuary 7 with nothing but your rifle.

Economy

Scrap is the soft currency for repairs and ammo. $AETHER is the on-chain utility token; in this slice, each extracted core pays out 25 AETHER into your local wallet.
