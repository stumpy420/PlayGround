HOW TO PLAY

01 · THE ROUND

A 3:00 bomb-defusal round. The infiltrators must plant at Site A before the clock runs out. The crew must finish 5 intel tasks, then defuse the bomb.

02 · THE PARANOIA

Two of the six operators are impostors in disguise. Witness a kill, find a body, and report it. Meetings freeze the round for a vote.

03 · THE CONTROLS

Left thumb moves. Right thumb aims and fires. USE interacts. KNIFE and LIGHTS appear if you are the impostor. Tilt aim is optional.

04 · THE WIN

Crew win by ejecting or killing both impostors, defusing the bomb, or surviving the round. Impostors win by detonation or by reaching parity.
