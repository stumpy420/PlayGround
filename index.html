HOW TO SURVIVE

01 / DEFUSE

Complete all six defusal tasks. Follow the amber markers and use the consoles.

One operator is a traitor. They kill quietly. Bodies and vents are evidence.

Report a body or call one emergency meeting. Read the comms, then vote.

Your sidearm kills in one shot — but shooting a crewmate loses the round. Only fire on proof.
