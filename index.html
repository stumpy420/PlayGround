How to Play

On foot

W A S D move · Shift sprint · Space jump · C climb steep ground and walls · swim by walking into deep water.

Battle

LMB strike · RMB block · 1–4 spells · B draw bow · R drink potion · Tab inventory.

The world

E talk, loot, enter · J quest log · M map · K skills · Esc pause.

Survival

Health does not regenerate quickly. Eat, drink, rest at shrines, and keep warm milk potions close. Blocking costs stamina. Spells cost mana.

The Ninth Life

You are the Tenth — the cat outside the nine. Follow the main quest to gather the Ember and the Bell, recruit companions, and descend beneath Frostfang.

Being a cat

Climb things. Knock objects off ledges. Sit in boxes. The guards will pretend not to notice. Everyone here is a cat, and they are all very serious about it.
